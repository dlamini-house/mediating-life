Our areas of practice

Space to be heard.
A path towards agreement.

Every dispute has its own story. We offer structured mediation to help you understand the issues, explore options and work towards a practical resolution.

01
Family law

Putting your family’s
future first.

Separation and family disagreements can be difficult to navigate. A mediator helps you discuss the decisions ahead and work towards clear arrangements.

  • Divorce settlement agreements
  • Child and spousal maintenance agreements
  • Parenting plans
  • Section 22 parental rights and responsibilities agreements

We create space for a structured conversation about your family’s needs and the practical steps forward.

02
Marital mediation

Make room for
a better conversation.

When communication becomes difficult, it can help to have a neutral person guide the discussion. Marital mediation gives couples space to identify concerns, listen to each other and explore mutually acceptable ways forward.

Our mediators use communication tools and negotiation techniques to support a constructive dialogue.

03
Civil & commercial mediation

Resolve the dispute.
Look ahead.

Disagreements can consume time and resources and put working relationships under strain. Mediation offers a structured setting for parties in civil or commercial disputes to discuss their concerns and explore possible solutions.

We focus on constructive communication and mutually beneficial agreements, with attention to the interests of everyone involved.

How mediation works

A little clarity

Before you begin.

How much does mediation cost?

Fees are discussed privately because every matter differs in complexity and time required. Sessions begin only once the invoice has been accepted.

Can I book an appointment online?

You can request a consultation through our contact page. The office must approve and confirm an appointment before it is booked.

Will mediation replace legal advice?

Mediation helps parties explore agreement. It does not replace independent legal advice. The steps required to formalise an agreement depend on your matter.

Not sure where to start?

Start with a conversation. Tell us a little about your situation and we’ll help you explore the next step.
