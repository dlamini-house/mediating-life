This page explains how the website’s contact options work.

Information you choose to send

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. The website does not submit or store the form contents. You decide whether to send the email. Information sent to Mediating Life is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the appropriate next steps.

Email and external services

Email, telephone and WhatsApp links open services outside this website. Their providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. Please avoid sending sensitive case documents in an initial enquiry.

Website resources

This site uses Google Fonts, which may receive connection information when the fonts load. No analytics or advertising scripts have been added. The website host may process technical request logs.

Questions about your information

Contact info@mediatinglife.co.za to ask about information you have provided, or to request a correction or deletion, subject to any applicable retention requirements.
